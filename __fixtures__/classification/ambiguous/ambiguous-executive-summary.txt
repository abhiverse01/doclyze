Nexora Health Technologies — FY2024 Annual Executive Summary

Prepared by: Office of the CEO
Date: January 15, 2025
Classification: Internal — Board Distribution

Overview
Nexora Health Technologies delivered exceptional performance in fiscal year 2024, achieving $342M in total revenue—a 28% year-over-year increase driven by strong adoption of our flagship remote patient monitoring platform, PulseLink Pro. We expanded our provider network to 2,800 healthcare systems across 14 countries, signed 47 enterprise contracts exceeding $1M in annual recurring revenue, and maintained a gross margin of 74%. This summary highlights key accomplishments, strategic initiatives, and forward-looking priorities.

Financial Highlights
Total Revenue: $342M (+28% YoY)
Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $298M (+34% YoY)
Gross Margin: 74% (stable YoY)
Operating Income: $41M (12% operating margin, up from 8% in FY2023)
Free Cash Flow: $28M
Total Headcount: 1,847 employees (+312 net hires)

Key Accomplishments
Product & Technology: Launched PulseLink Pro 3.0 in Q2, featuring AI-powered early warning alerts for sepsis and cardiac deterioration. The algorithm, validated across 12 clinical trials with 15,000+ patients, demonstrated a sensitivity of 94.2% and a false positive rate below 3%. We filed 14 new patents during the fiscal year and were granted 9. Our engineering team reduced average alert response time by 62% through a redesigned real-time notification pipeline.

Market Expansion: Entered the Japanese and Australian markets through strategic partnerships with Sony Health and Medtel AU, respectively. Japan contributed $18M in revenue within the first six months of launch. Our European operations, headquartered in Amsterdam, grew 41% year-over-year to $97M, now representing 28% of total revenue.

Clinical Outcomes: Peer-reviewed studies published in JAMA Digital Health and The Lancet Digital Health demonstrated that PulseLink Pro reduced 30-day hospital readmissions by 19% and average length of stay by 1.4 days across our deployed hospital network. These outcomes directly contributed to our Net Promoter Score increasing from 62 to 78 among provider clients.

Strategic Priorities for FY2025
1. AI-Driven Personalization: Invest $45M in developing patient-specific predictive models that integrate genomics, wearable data, and electronic health records to enable truly personalized remote monitoring.
2. Value-Based Care Partnerships: Expand our payer relationships from 4 to 12 health insurance plans, targeting risk-sharing arrangements where Nexora's technology reduces total cost of care.
3. Operational Excellence: Target 15% improvement in customer onboarding time through process automation and dedicated implementation teams.
4. International Growth: Pursue market entry in Brazil, India, and South Korea, with a target of $60M in incremental international revenue.

Leadership & Culture
In FY2024, we were recognized as a "Best Workplace in Healthcare Technology" by Modern Healthcare magazine for the third consecutive year. Employee retention improved to 91% (up from 86%). We launched the Nexora Academy, an internal learning platform offering 200+ courses in clinical informatics, software engineering, and leadership development. Our diversity initiative resulted in women representing 42% of all new hires and 38% of leadership positions.

Risk Factors
Key risks include increasing regulatory scrutiny of AI in healthcare (particularly the EU AI Act's requirements for high-risk medical devices), potential reimbursement changes under evolving CMS policies, and competitive pressure from both established medtech companies and well-funded startups. Our regulatory affairs team is actively engaged with the FDA's Digital Health Center of Excellence and European notified bodies to ensure compliance across all markets.
