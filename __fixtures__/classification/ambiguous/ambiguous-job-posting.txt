SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER — PLATFORM INFRASTRUCTURE

About the Role

We're looking for a Senior Software Engineer to join our Platform Infrastructure team. You'll design and build the systems that power our real-time collaboration features used by millions of professionals worldwide. This is a high-impact role reporting directly to the VP of Engineering.

What You'll Do

- Architect and implement scalable microservices in Go and Rust that handle 500K+ concurrent WebSocket connections
- Lead technical design reviews and mentor junior and mid-level engineers
- Design and optimize our event-driven architecture using Apache Kafka and Redis Streams
- Build and operate observability infrastructure (metrics, logging, tracing) using Prometheus, Grafana, and OpenTelemetry
- Collaborate with product managers and designers to translate requirements into technical solutions
- Participate in on-call rotation and incident response

What We're Looking For

- 5+ years of professional experience in backend systems engineering
- Strong proficiency in Go, Rust, Python, or Java
- Experience with distributed systems, including consensus protocols, partitioning, and replication
- Deep understanding of networking (TCP/IP, HTTP/2, WebSockets)
- Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP preferred)
- Excellent communication skills and the ability to explain complex technical concepts

Bonus Points

- Experience building real-time collaboration systems (CRDTs, OT)
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Experience with eBPF and kernel-level performance optimization
- Previous experience at a high-growth startup

What We Offer

- Competitive salary: $200,000 – $280,000 base + equity
- Comprehensive benefits: medical, dental, vision, 401(k) with 4% match
- Unlimited PTO (with a 3-week minimum)
- $5,000 annual learning and development budget
- Home office stipend of $2,000
- Relocation assistance available
