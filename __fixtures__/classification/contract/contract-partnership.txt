STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Strategic Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of September 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

PARTY A: Nordic Wellness AB, a Swedish aktiebolag registered in Stockholm, Sweden ("Nordic"), registration number 556702-3847.

PARTY B: Pacific Health Brands Inc., a California corporation ("Pacific"), with offices at 340 Brannan Street, San Francisco, CA 94107.

WHEREAS, Nordic manufactures premium wellness and recovery products including cold plunge tubs, infrared saunas, and recovery equipment, sold primarily in the European market; and

WHEREAS, Pacific operates a direct-to-consumer e-commerce platform and wholesale distribution network for wellness products in the United States and Canada;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to establish a strategic partnership for the exclusive distribution and co-branding of Nordic's products in North America.

1. PARTNERSHIP STRUCTURE
1.1 Pacific shall serve as Nordic's exclusive distributor in the United States and Canada for the product categories listed in Exhibit A (the "Territory" and the "Products").
1.2 The Parties shall jointly develop co-branded products combining Nordic's hardware expertise with Pacific's brand and distribution capabilities.
1.3 This Agreement does not create a joint venture, partnership, or agency relationship between the Parties. Each Party is an independent contractor.

2. EXCLUSIVITY
2.1 Pacific shall have exclusive distribution rights in the Territory for the Products during the Initial Term (defined in Section 6).
2.2 Nordic shall not sell, distribute, or authorize any third party to sell or distribute the Products in the Territory during the exclusivity period.
2.3 Pacific shall meet minimum purchase requirements of $2,000,000 in the first year, $3,500,000 in the second year, and $5,000,000 in the third year.

3. PRICING AND PAYMENT
3.1 Wholesale Pricing: Nordic shall sell Products to Pacific at 45% of the European retail price (MSRP), converted to USD at the ECB reference rate on the date of each order.
3.2 Co-branded Products: Revenue shall be split 60% to Pacific and 40% to Nordic, net of manufacturing and logistics costs.
3.3 Payment Terms: Net 60 days from invoice date for all orders.
3.4 Price Adjustments: Nordic may adjust wholesale prices annually by up to the lesser of 5% or CPI increase, with 90 days' prior written notice.

4. MARKETING AND BRAND GUIDELINES
4.1 Pacific shall market the Products in compliance with Nordic's brand guidelines, which shall be provided in writing.
4.2 Pacific shall invest a minimum of $200,000 annually in marketing and promotional activities for the Products.
4.3 All co-branded marketing materials require mutual written approval prior to publication.

5. QUALITY AND WARRANTY
5.1 Nordic warrants that all Products shall conform to applicable product specifications and be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of two (2) years from the date of sale to the end consumer.
5.2 Nordic shall indemnify Pacific against product liability claims arising from defects in the Products.
5.3 All Products must comply with applicable U.S. and Canadian safety standards, including UL certification and CSA certification.

6. TERM
6.1 Initial Term: Three (3) years from the Effective Date.
6.2 Renewal: Automatic renewal for successive two (2) year periods unless either Party provides 180 days' notice of non-renewal.

7. TERMINATION
7.1 Either Party may terminate for material breach uncured after 60 days' written notice.
7.2 Pacific may terminate if Nordic fails to deliver Products within 90 days of the agreed shipment date.
7.3 Upon termination, Pacific may sell remaining inventory for 180 days, after which unsold inventory may be returned to Nordic at Nordic's cost.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California, USA.
8.2 Any disputes shall be resolved first by good-faith negotiation, then by binding arbitration in San Francisco under the ICC Rules.
