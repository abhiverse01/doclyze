CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 15, 2024, by and between:

CLIENT: Greenfield Solar Holdings LLC ("Client"), a Delaware limited liability company with offices at 1550 Wynkoop Street, Suite 500, Denver, CO 80202.

CONSULTANT: Dr. Margaret Ellison ("Consultant"), an individual with an address of 2840 Pearl Street, Boulder, CO 80302.

1. ENGAGEMENT
Client hereby engages Consultant to provide environmental impact assessment and regulatory compliance consulting services in connection with Client's proposed 200MW solar farm project in Pueblo County, Colorado (the "Project").

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Consultant shall perform the following services:
2.1 Conduct a comprehensive environmental impact assessment (EIA) for the Project site, including flora and fauna surveys, wetland delineation, and cultural resource assessment, in accordance with NEPA and Colorado state requirements.
2.2 Prepare and file all required environmental permit applications, including the Colorado Department of Public Health and Environment (CDPHE) 1041 permit and Army Corps of Engineers Section 404 permit.
2.3 Serve as the Project's environmental representative in all regulatory proceedings, public hearings, and agency consultations.
2.4 Develop and implement an environmental monitoring and mitigation plan for the construction and operational phases of the Project.
2.5 Provide up to 40 hours per month of ad-hoc environmental advisory services.

3. TERM
The initial term of this engagement shall be eighteen (18) months, commencing May 15, 2024, and ending November 14, 2025. The term may be extended by mutual written agreement.

4. COMPENSATION
4.1 Monthly Retainer: Client shall pay Consultant a monthly retainer fee of $12,500, payable on the first business day of each calendar month.
4.2 Hourly Rate: For services exceeding 40 hours in any calendar month, Consultant shall be compensated at a rate of $325 per hour.
4.3 Expenses: Client shall reimburse Consultant for pre-approved, reasonable out-of-pocket expenses including travel, lodging, subcontractor fees, and specialized testing costs. Expenses exceeding $5,000 require prior written approval.
4.4 Payment Terms: Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt.

5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS
Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture. Consultant is responsible for all applicable taxes and insurance.

6. DELIVERABLES
6.1 Draft EIA Report: Within 6 months of the Effective Date.
6.2 Final EIA Report: Within 9 months, incorporating agency feedback.
6.3 Permit Applications: Filed within 10 months.
6.4 Monthly Status Reports: Submitted by the 5th business day of each month.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Consultant shall not disclose any proprietary information about the Project, including financial projections, land acquisition details, and interconnection agreements, to any third party without Client's prior written consent.

8. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon 30 days' written notice. Client shall pay Consultant for all services performed and expenses incurred through the termination date.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.
