EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the “Effective Date”), by and between:

EMPLOYER: Pinnacle Therapeutics Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 245 First Street, Suite 1800, Cambridge, MA 02142 (“Company”)

EMPLOYEE: Dr. Amanda Foster, an individual residing at 42 Adams Road, Brookline, MA 02446 (“Employee”)

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
1.1 Company hereby employs Employee as Vice President of Clinical Development, reporting to the Chief Medical Officer.
1.2 Employee’s duties shall include: overseeing all Phase I-III clinical trials; managing relationships with contract research organizations (CROs); preparing regulatory submissions to the FDA and EMA; leading the clinical data analysis team; and presenting clinical data at scientific conferences and to the Board of Directors.
1.3 Employee shall devote her full professional time and attention to the business of the Company. Employee may engage in outside activities (including academic affiliations) with the prior written consent of the CMO, provided such activities do not conflict with her duties.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 Base Salary: Company shall pay Employee a base salary of $295,000 per year, payable in bi-weekly installments in accordance with the Company’s standard payroll schedule.
2.2 Annual Bonus: Employee shall be eligible for an annual performance bonus with a target of 40% of base salary ($118,000) and a maximum of 60% of base salary ($177,000). Bonus payment is subject to the Company’s bonus plan and the achievement of individual and company performance goals.
2.3 Equity: Employee shall receive a stock option grant to purchase 45,000 shares of Company common stock at an exercise price equal to the fair market value on the grant date, vesting over four (4) years with a one-year cliff, subject to the Company’s 2024 Equity Incentive Plan.
2.4 Sign-On Bonus: Employee shall receive a one-time sign-on bonus of $50,000, payable within 30 days of the Effective Date. Employee must repay the full sign-on bonus if she voluntarily resigns within 12 months of the Effective Date.

3. BENEFITS
Employee shall be eligible to participate in all benefit programs offered by the Company to its executive employees, including: (a) medical, dental, and vision insurance; (b) 401(k) plan with 4% company match; (c) 20 days paid time off per year; (d) 12 company holidays; (e) short-term and long-term disability insurance; (f) group life insurance at 2x salary.

4. TERMINATION
4.1 At-Will: Employment is at-will and may be terminated by either Party at any time, with or without cause.
4.2 Severance: If Employee is terminated without Cause (as defined) or resigns for Good Reason (as defined), Employee shall receive: (a) six (6) months of base salary continuation; (b) accelerated vesting of any unvested options granted in the most recent grant; (c) continuation of health insurance for six (6) months under COBRA.

5. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
5.1 During employment and for twelve (12) months following termination, Employee shall not engage in competitive activities within the Company’s therapeutic areas (oncology and immunology) in North America and Europe.
5.2 During employment and for eighteen (18) months following termination, Employee shall not solicit or hire any Company employee.

6. INVENTION ASSIGNMENT
Employee assigns to Company all rights to any inventions conceived or reduced to practice during employment that relate to the Company’s business.

7. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
