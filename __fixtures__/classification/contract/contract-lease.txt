COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made and entered into as of February 1, 2024, by and between:

LANDLORD: Metropolitan Properties LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord"), whose address is 1000 Wilshire Boulevard, Suite 500, Los Angeles, CA 90017.

TENANT: Lumina Studios Inc., a California corporation ("Tenant"), whose address for notices is 1000 Wilshire Boulevard, Suite 1500, Los Angeles, CA 90017 (the "Premises").

1. LEASED PREMISES
1.1 Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, approximately 6,200 rentable square feet of office space located on the 15th floor of the building known as 1000 Wilshire Boulevard, Los Angeles, CA 90017 (the "Building").
1.2 Tenant shall also have the non-exclusive right to use common areas including lobbies, elevators, restrooms, and parking garage (subject to Section 5).

2. LEASE TERM
2.1 Initial Term: Five (5) years, commencing February 1, 2024 ("Commencement Date") and expiring January 31, 2029.
2.2 Option to Renew: Tenant shall have two (2) successive options to renew for five (5) years each, provided Tenant is not in default at the time of exercise. Tenant must provide written notice of intent to renew at least twelve (12) months prior to expiration.

3. BASE RENT
3.1 Base Rent shall be as follows:
    - Year 1 (02/01/2024 – 01/31/2025): $48,100/month ($93.25/RSF/year)
    - Year 2 (02/01/2025 – 01/31/2026): $49,463/month ($96.05/RSF/year)
    - Year 3 (02/01/2026 – 01/31/2027): $50,947/month ($98.93/RSF/year)
    - Year 4 (02/01/2027 – 01/31/2028): $52,475/month ($101.90/RSF/year)
    - Year 5 (02/01/2028 – 01/31/2029): $54,050/month ($104.95/RSF/year)
3.2 Base Rent is due on the first day of each calendar month.

4. ADDITIONAL RENT
4.1 Tenant shall pay Tenant's Pro Rata Share (estimated at 4.8%) of Operating Expenses and Property Taxes, payable in monthly installments based on Landlord's annual budget, with annual true-up.
4.2 Tenant shall pay Tenant's Pro Rata Share of insurance costs for the Building.
4.3 Estimated Monthly Additional Rent (Year 1): $8,400/month.

5. PARKING
Tenant is allocated twelve (12) unreserved parking spaces in the Building garage at the rate of $275 per space per month.

6. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall provide a security deposit of $96,200 (two months' Base Rent) upon execution of this Lease.

7. USE
Premises shall be used and occupied solely for general office purposes and a film editing studio, and for no other purpose.

8. ALTERATIONS
Tenant may not make any alterations costing more than $10,000 in the aggregate without Landlord's prior written consent. All alterations shall become Landlord's property upon termination.

9. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
If Tenant fails to pay rent within ten (10) days of the due date, or fails to cure any other default within thirty (30) days after notice, Landlord may terminate this Lease, re-enter the Premises, and recover all damages.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
