Model KC-4520 Combination Microwave Oven with Convection

READ ALL INSTRUCTIONS BEFORE USE

Congratulations on your purchase of the KitchenCraft KC-4520. This appliance combines the speed of microwave cooking with the browning and crisping capabilities of a convection oven, giving you versatility for a wide range of dishes.

SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

WARNING: To reduce the risk of fire, electric shock, or injury:
- Do not operate the oven empty. This can damage the magnetron.
- Do not use metal utensils, aluminum foil, or metal-trimmed dishes in microwave mode.
- Do not attempt to cook eggs in their shells. Pressure buildup may cause the eggs to explode.
- Do not heat sealed containers or closed plastic bags.
- Do not use the oven for storage.
- Do not operate the oven if the door is damaged or does not close properly.
- Do not clean the oven with abrasive cleaners or steel wool.
- Children should not use the oven without adult supervision.

BEFORE FIRST USE
1. Remove all packaging materials and the accessory pack from the oven cavity.
2. Wash the turntable, roller ring, and wire rack in warm soapy water.
3. Wipe the interior with a damp cloth.
4. Place the oven on a flat, stable surface with at least 4 inches of clearance on all sides.
5. Plug into a dedicated 120V, 15A grounded outlet.

CONTROL PANEL OVERVIEW

1. DISPLAY SCREEN: Shows time of day, cooking time, power level, and temperature.
2. MICROWAVE BUTTON: Activates microwave cooking mode. Press repeatedly to cycle through power levels (High, 70%, 50%, 30%, 10%).
3. CONVECTION BUTTON: Activates convection oven mode. Use +/- buttons to set temperature (200°F to 450°F).
4. COMBI BUTTON: Activates combination cooking (microwave + convection simultaneously).
5. TIMER BUTTON: Set cooking duration using the numeric keypad.
6. START/+30 SEC: Starts cooking. Adds 30 seconds when pressed during microwave operation.
7. STOP/CLEAR: Cancels current operation or clears programming.
8. POWER LEVEL: Adjusts microwave power (P1 through P10).
9. DEFROST: Activates the auto-defrost cycle. Enter food weight in pounds using the keypad.
10. SENSOR REHEAT: Automatically determines reheating time based on steam detection.

BASIC OPERATIONS

Microwave Cooking:
1. Place food in a microwave-safe container on the turntable.
2. Press MICROWAVE. The default power is HIGH (P10).
3. Enter cooking time (e.g., press 3-0-0 for 3 minutes).
4. Press START.

Convection Baking:
1. Place the wire rack on the turntable.
2. Place food in an oven-safe dish on the rack.
3. Press CONVECTION.
4. Set desired temperature (e.g., 3-7-5 for 375°F).
5. Enter cooking time.
6. Press START. The oven will preheat before beginning the cooking cycle.

MAINTENANCE
- Clean the interior after each use with a damp cloth and mild detergent.
- The turntable and roller ring are dishwasher safe.
- If odors persist, place a bowl of water with 2 tablespoons of lemon juice inside and run on HIGH for 5 minutes.
- Unplug the oven before cleaning the exterior.
