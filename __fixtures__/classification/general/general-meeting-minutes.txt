MEETING MINUTES

Board of Directors Meeting
River City Community Foundation

Date: March 12, 2024
Time: 5:30 PM – 7:15 PM
Location: Foundation Conference Room, 450 NW Davis Street, Portland, OR 97209

ATTENDEES
Present: Maria Santos (Chair), David Chen (Vice Chair), Patricia Vega (Secretary), Robert Tanaka (Treasurer), Dr. Amanda Foster, James Wilson
Absent: Thomas Reyes
Staff Present: Karen Mitchell (Executive Director), Brian Coughlin (Director of Programs)

CALL TO ORDER
Chair Santos called the meeting to order at 5:32 PM. A quorum was present.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES
Motion by Tanaka, seconded by Foster, to approve the minutes of the February 13, 2024 meeting. Approved unanimously.

EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR'S REPORT
Ms. Mitchell presented the following updates:

1. Fundraising: The annual gala held on February 24 raised $892,000 in gross revenue, a 34% increase over 2023. Net proceeds after event costs were $684,000.

2. Grantmaking: The spring grant cycle received 47 applications requesting a total of $4.2 million. The grants committee recommended funding 18 organizations for a total of $1.8 million. A summary of recommended grants was distributed.

3. Staffing: The Director of Development position remains open after two months of recruitment. Three final candidates have been identified; interviews are scheduled for next week.

4. Building Renovation: The kitchen renovation in the community center is 80% complete. Expected completion is April 15. Total cost is tracking $12,000 (6%) under budget.

FINANCIAL REPORT
Mr. Tanaka presented the February 2024 financial report:
- Total revenue YTD: $3.2M (budget: $3.0M)
- Total expenses YTD: $2.8M (budget: $2.9M)
- Investment portfolio return YTD: +4.2%
- Operating reserve: 7.2 months (target: 6 months)

Motion by Vega, seconded by Wilson, to accept the financial report. Approved unanimously.

OLD BUSINESS

1. Scholarship Program Expansion: Dr. Foster reported that the scholarship committee has completed its review of 120 applications. 35 scholarships will be awarded totaling $420,000 for the 2024-2025 academic year.

NEW BUSINESS

1. Strategic Plan Update: Ms. Mitchell presented a draft of the 2025-2028 strategic plan. Key priorities include expanding the youth mentorship program to three new school districts, establishing a donor-advised fund program, and increasing the endowment from $28.9M to $35M by 2028.

2. Partnership Proposal: Mr. Chen introduced a partnership proposal from Pacific Northwest Housing Authority to co-fund a $1.5M affordable housing initiative. Board directed Ms. Mitchell to negotiate terms and present a formal proposal at the April meeting.

ADJOURNMENT
Motion by Wilson, seconded by Tanaka, to adjourn at 7:15 PM. Approved unanimously.

Next Meeting: April 9, 2024 at 5:30 PM

Respectfully submitted,
Patricia Vega, Secretary
