SMOKED PAPRIKA CHICKEN WITH CHORIZO AND CHICKPEA STEW

Serves 4 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 35 min

This one-pot stew is my go-to weeknight dinner when I want something that feels special but doesn't require a trip to a specialty grocery store. The smoked paprika gives it a deep, almost campfire-like warmth, and the chorizo releases enough fat to sauté everything else in the pot. Serve it with crusty bread and a simple green salad.

INGREDIENTS

4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2 lbs total)
1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
1/2 tsp black pepper
2 tbsp olive oil
6 oz Spanish chorizo, sliced into 1/2-inch rounds
1 large yellow onion, diced
3 garlic cloves, minced
1 tbsp smoked paprika (Pimentón de la Vera)
1/2 tsp ground cumin
1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
1 can (15 oz) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
1/2 cup chicken broth
1 cup frozen peas
2 cups baby spinach
1 tbsp sherry vinegar or red wine vinegar
Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

INSTRUCTIONS

1. Pat the chicken thighs dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.

2. Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Place the chicken skin-side down and cook without moving for 6-7 minutes, until the skin is deeply golden brown and crispy. Flip and cook 4-5 minutes more. Remove the chicken to a plate. It won't be fully cooked yet—that's fine.

3. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the chorizo slices to the rendered chicken fat and cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chorizo has released its oil and is slightly crispy at the edges. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the chorizo to the plate with the chicken.

4. Add the diced onion to the pot. Cook for 4-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and beginning to caramelize. Add the garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, and cayenne. Stir constantly for 30 seconds until fragrant—be careful not to let the paprika burn.

5. Pour in the diced tomatoes with their juice, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Add the chickpeas and chicken broth. Stir to combine. Nestle the chicken thighs back into the stew, skin-side up.

6. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low. Cover and cook for 20-25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through (internal temperature 165°F) and the sauce has thickened slightly.

7. Remove the chicken to a cutting board. Stir the peas and spinach into the stew—they'll wilt in about 2 minutes. Stir in the sherry vinegar.

8. Shred the chicken meat from the bone (discard skin and bones if desired, though the crispy skin is the best part) and return it to the pot. Add the reserved chorizo. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.

9. Serve in shallow bowls, garnished with parsley and crusty bread on the side.

WINE PAIRING: A medium-bodied Spanish red like a Ribera del Duero or a young Rioja Crianza. If you prefer white, a Viura from Rioja works beautifully.
