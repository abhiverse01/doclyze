EMPLOYEE REMOTE WORK POLICY

Policy Number: HR-2024-012
Effective Date: April 1, 2024
Applies To: All full-time and part-time employees
Approved By: Chief People Officer

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements at Meridian Technologies Inc., balancing operational needs with employee flexibility. Remote work is a privilege, not an entitlement, and may be modified or revoked based on business requirements.

2. ELIGIBILITY
2.1 Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period and received a "Meets Expectations" or higher performance rating are eligible to request remote work.
2.2 Roles that require physical presence (lab work, facilities management, on-site IT support) are generally not eligible for remote work arrangements.
2.3 Remote work arrangements are subject to manager approval and must be documented in the company's HRIS system.

3. REMOTE WORK MODELS
3.1 Fully Remote: Employee works from a location other than a Meridian office 100% of the time. Requires VP-level approval.
3.2 Hybrid: Employee works from a Meridian office 2-3 days per week and remotely on remaining days. This is the default model for eligible roles.
3.3 Temporary Remote: Short-term remote arrangement (up to 30 days) for personal or family circumstances. Requires manager approval only.

4. WORKSPACE REQUIREMENTS
4.1 Employees must maintain a dedicated workspace that is free from distractions, sufficiently quiet for video calls, and ergonomically appropriate.
4.2 The company provides a one-time $1,000 home office stipend for approved fully remote and hybrid employees, to be used for desk, chair, monitor, or other approved equipment. Receipts must be submitted within 90 days.
4.3 High-speed internet (minimum 50 Mbps download) is required.

5. AVAILABILITY AND COMMUNICATION
5.1 Remote employees must be available during core business hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM in their primary office's time zone) unless otherwise agreed with their manager.
5.2 Employees must respond to Slack messages and emails within 2 hours during business hours.
5.3 Cameras must be on during all video meetings unless the employee has a documented accommodation.

6. EXPENSES AND EQUIPMENT
6.1 The company will provide a laptop and any required peripherals.
6.2 Monthly internet subsidy of $75 for fully remote employees; $40 for hybrid employees.
6.3 The company does not reimburse for home utility costs, general home maintenance, or office supplies.

7. DATA SECURITY
7.1 Remote employees must use the company-provided VPN for all work activities.
7.2 Screens must be locked when unattended.
7.3 Work devices must not be used by household members.
7.4 Printing of confidential documents at home is prohibited.

8. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Violations of this policy may result in modification or revocation of remote work privileges and/or disciplinary action up to and including termination.
