SERVICE DESCRIPTION

This purchase order covers clinical trial management services for Study PTX-204 (Phase II, Nilotinib in Parkinson's Disease) at the following 6 clinical sites:

1. Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston, MA
2. UCSF Medical Center, San Francisco, CA
3. Cleveland Clinic, Cleveland, OH
4. University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, PA
5. Karolinska Institute, Stockholm, Sweden
6. Charité University Hospital, Berlin, Germany
