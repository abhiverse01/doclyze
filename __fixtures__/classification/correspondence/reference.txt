Dear Members of the Admissions Committee,

I am writing to provide a character and academic reference for Ms. Aarati Shrestha, who is applying to the MSc Computer Science program at the University of Oxford. I have known Aarati for three years in my capacity as her undergraduate thesis supervisor and professor of algorithms and data structures at Tribhuvan University.

Aarati is among the top five percent of students I have taught in my twelve-year academic career. Her undergraduate thesis on "Optimizing Graph Traversal Algorithms for Large-Scale Social Network Analysis" demonstrated exceptional analytical depth and originality. She independently designed a novel heuristic that reduced traversal time by 30% on datasets exceeding one million nodes.

Beyond her technical abilities, Aarati possesses outstanding personal qualities. She is disciplined, self-motivated, and possesses a genuine intellectual curiosity that extends beyond the classroom. She regularly contributed to our department's research seminars and mentored junior students in data structures and competitive programming.

I have no doubt that Aarati will thrive in a rigorous graduate program. She has the intellectual foundation, work ethic, and character to make meaningful contributions to your academic community. I recommend her without reservation.
